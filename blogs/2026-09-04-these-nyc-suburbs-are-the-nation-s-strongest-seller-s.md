Three of the country’s six seller’s markets are New York City suburbs: Long Island, NY; Northern NJ; and Central NJ. Long Island is the strongest seller’s market in the country, with 36.2% fewer sellers than buyers, followed by Northern NJ (20.7%). Nearby Central NJ (12.9%) is the 5th-strongest seller’s market.
