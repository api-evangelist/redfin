Delistings reached near-record levels in April, with 5.8% of listings withdrawn — matching December 2025 highs and rivaling pandemic-era peaks. Simultaneously, relistings hit their highest share since mid-2020, as sellers who previously delisted attempt second listings after accepting market realities. Atlanta leads in delistings while Bay Area sellers dominate relistings.
