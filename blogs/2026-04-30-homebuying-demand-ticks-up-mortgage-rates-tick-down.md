<p><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">More homeowners are putting their houses on the market, with new listings increasing for the second week in a row after five straight months of declines. </span></i></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">U.S. pending home sales rose 2.7% year over year during the four weeks ending April 26, the biggest increase in six weeks. Mortgage-purchase applications have risen to their </span><a href="https://www.investing.com/economic-calendar/mba-purchase-index-1494"><span style="font-weight: 400;">highest level</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> in three months. </span></p>
<p><b>There are a few reasons more homebuyers are on the hunt:</b></p>
<ul>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><b>Affordability is improving. </b><span style="font-weight: 400;">The weekly average mortgage rate has dropped to </span><a href="https://www.freddiemac.com/pmms"><span style="font-weight: 400;">6.23%</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> from a seven-month high of 6.46% at the start of April, pushing the median monthly housing payment down 2.2% year over year. </span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><b>Markets have started to stabilize. </b><span style="font-weight: 400;">At the same time, some sidelined house hunters are encouraged because markets–and mortgage rates–have steadied as the Iran war </span><a href="https://www.redfin.com/news/weekly-economic-update-april-27-2026/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">fades a bit</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> from the headlines. </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">The timing of Easter is also contributing to the year-over-year uptick in pending sales; Easter didn’t fall into this four-week period, but it was in 2025’s comparable period.</span></li>
</ul>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Sellers are coming out of the woodwork as they notice demand from buyers creeping up. New listings rose 2.2% year over year, the second week of increases after five straight months of declines. We are also in the midst of </span><a href="https://www.redfin.com/news/best-time-to-sell-buy-home/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">prime home-selling season</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">, when homes are more likely to sell above their asking price, and to sell faster. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">“Even though more buyers are coming off the sidelines, some are still wondering if they should wait for mortgage rates to fall more before making a move. I tell them no–if you love a home and you can afford it, make an offer,” said <a href="https://www.redfin.com/real-estate-agents/sue-dhillon">Sue Dhillon</a>, a Redfin Premier agent in </span><a href="https://www.redfin.com/real-estate-agents/sue-dhillon"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Seattle</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">. “It is a </span><a href="https://www.redfin.com/news/buyers-vs-sellers-march-2026/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">buyer’s market</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">, but there is competition for desirable homes in popular neighborhoods. And while prices are lower than they have been over the past few years, they will rise quickly if bidding wars do pick up.”</span></p>
<p><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">For Redfin economists’ takes on the housing market, please visit Redfin’s “</span></i><a href="https://www.redfin.com/news/from-our-economists/"><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">From Our Economists</span></i></a><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">” page. </span></i></p>
