More than 1,200 Redfin agents earned recognition in RealTrends Verified rankings, placing them in the top 1.5% of real estate professionals nationally. These agents collectively achieved $22.9 billion in sales volume and nearly 34,000 transactions in 2025, operating successfully despite affordability pressures and market uncertainty that tested both buyers and sellers.
