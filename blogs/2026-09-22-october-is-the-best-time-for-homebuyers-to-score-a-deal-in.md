Early October is typically the best time for homebuyers to score a deal in Boston, Chicago, and a few other U.S. metros, according to a Home Economics and Redfin analysis. For buyers in Philadelphia, Virginia Beach and a handful of other places, mid-October may be the best time to get a bargain.
