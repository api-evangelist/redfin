<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Surrounded by the hustle and bustle of Lower Manhattan, this </span><a href="https://www.redfin.com/NY/New-York/150-Nassau-St-10038/unit-2A/home/45124291"><span style="font-weight: 400;">two-bedroom, two-and-a-half-bathroom loft-style apartment</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> is listed for $2,199,000. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Located a block from City Hall Park and close to the Brooklyn Bridge too, this 1,700 square-foot apartment has 13-foot ceilings and 11-foot arched windows. It is what listing agent </span><a href="https://www.compass.com/agents/eithan-davidov/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Eithan Davidov</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> from Compass called a “one-of-a-kind-apartment.”</span></p>
<figure class="wp-caption alignnone" id="attachment_85002" style="width: 1800px;"><img alt="" class="wp-image-85002 size-full" height="1200" src="https://www.redfin.com/news/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/NPM8877-scaled.jpg" width="1800" /><figcaption class="wp-caption-text" id="caption-attachment-85002">The apartment has two bedrooms and two-and-a-half bathrooms. Photo credit: Nathan Patrick</figcaption></figure>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">“You walk in and you feel the vibe because the windows are gorgeous and the high ceilings are breathtaking. It is a unique opportunity because it’s one of the largest apartments you could own at this price point in City Hall Park,” Davidov told Redfin News. “The location lets you have everything at your fingertips too.”</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">The apartment is just a couple blocks away from major subway lines and landmarks like Wall St, City Hall, South Street Seaport and Battery Park. </span></p>
<figure class="wp-caption alignnone" id="attachment_85001" style="width: 1500px;"><img alt="" class="wp-image-85001 size-full" height="1000" src="https://www.redfin.com/news/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/150-Nassau-St-Unit-2AKITCHEN1.jpg" width="1500" /><figcaption class="wp-caption-text" id="caption-attachment-85001">The kitchen has a breakfast bar, dishwasher and wine fridge. Photo credit: Nathan Patrick</figcaption></figure>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">The apartment is located inside the Park Place Tower, which was originally built as the headquarters of the American Tract Society in the late 1890s. It was last an office for the former newspaper, “New York Sun,” before it was converted into apartments from 1999 to 2002 and was designated a New York City landmark around the same time. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">“In this apartment you get the industrial vibe from the old life of the building while still having an airy and open space,” he said. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">The property also has a private terrace that allows for indoor-outdoor living without ever leaving the comfort of your home. The unit also has a washer and dryer, custom built-in bookshelves and recessed lighting throughout. </span></p>

<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">“The ideal buyer for this place is someone who knows what this part of town has to offer and will appreciate the space and the convenience,” Davidov added. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">The building also offers residents full-service amenities, including a 24-hour doorman, a health club, common library/lounge and a shared rooftop deck.</span></p>
