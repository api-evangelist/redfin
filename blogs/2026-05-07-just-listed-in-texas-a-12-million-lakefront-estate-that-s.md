In this edition of Just Listed–a series from Redfin News that explores listings that are listed by Redfin or Compass agents on Redfin.com–we’re exploring a Mediterranean-inspired estate in Austin, Texas. Nestled along the shores of Lake Austin in the coveted Rivercrest neighborhood of Austin, TX, this stunning $12 million estate blends luxury living with unbeatable waterfront views. It has four-bedrooms and five-bathrooms, and a standout fireplace in the living room. It has been on the market for just a few days and won’t last long, said Compass agent Cathy Romano, who is listing the property.
