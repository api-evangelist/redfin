New home listings dropped 1.3% week-over-week, marking one of 2026's steepest declines as prospective sellers retreat amid elevated mortgage rates and buyer hesitation. With rates climbing to their highest level since August and housing payments approaching peak levels, economic uncertainty is keeping both buyers and sellers cautious about market participation.
