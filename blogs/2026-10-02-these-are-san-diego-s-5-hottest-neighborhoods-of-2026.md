Otay Ranch is San Diego’s hottest neighborhood of 2026, followed by San Pasqual Valley and Hillcrest. The list spans from neighborhoods near downtown San Diego to further inland. San Diego’s hottest neighborhoods offer a mix of relatively affordable homes, outdoor amenities and access to restaurants and shopping.
